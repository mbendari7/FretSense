# Design System: FretSense

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Luthier's Bench"**

FretSense looks like a well-kept workbench. The surroundings are plain, cool and orderly: slate, paper and a single blue. The instrument on the bench is the only rich material in the room: a rosewood board, nickel frets, a bone nut and pearl inlays. Colour that carries meaning appears only as note markers on that board, and those markers use the Okabe-Ito palette so they stay distinguishable for colour-blind players.

The interface is an Operate surface (explorer, drills, gear, settings) with Read pockets (lessons, handbook). Density is moderate and touch-first: every target is at least 44px, controls sit in wrapped rows, and the fretboard is the widest and most detailed thing on any screen. It follows the system light or dark preference, which the user can override, and offers three text sizes that scale the whole type ramp through one `--step` variable.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One neutral frame (slate top bar, icon rail, paper canvas) and one accent blue for actions and links.
- The fretboard is drawn as a real instrument in SVG: rosewood, nickel, bone, pearl.
- Okabe-Ito markers for root, tone, reference, correct, incorrect and revealed. State is never shown by colour alone.
- One humanist, high-legibility typeface (Atkinson Hyperlegible Next) at two weights.
- Flat surfaces, 1px borders, two radii. No shadows.

## Colors

The neutrals are a cool, desaturated slate, and the colour sits on the instrument and in its markers.

### Primary
- **Okabe Blue** (`accent`): links, primary buttons and the selected state of form accents. In dark mode it lightens to Sky (`accent-dark`) with dark ink on top.

### Neutral
- **Workshop Slate** (`slate`): the top bar, and black-key root buttons on the explorer.
- **Paper** (`paper`) / **Night Paper** (`paper-dark`): the page canvas, and the fill of tags and chips.
- **Surface** (`surface`) / **Night Surface** (`surface-dark`): cards, inputs, buttons and the active rail item.
- **Ink** (`ink`) and **Soft Ink** (`ink-soft`): body text and secondary text (captions, degrees, metadata).
- **Hairline** (`line`): every 1px border, divider and table rule.

### Instrument materials
- **Rosewood** (`rosewood`, edged with `rosewood-edge`): the fingerboard, and the brand mark.
- **Nickel** (`nickel-fret`), **Bone** (`bone-nut`), **String** (`string`), **Pearl** (`pearl`): frets, nut, strings and inlays. These appear only on the drawn instrument.

### Markers (Okabe-Ito, FR-013)
- **Vermilion Root** (`marker-root`): the root note, the active rail indicator, the selected root and chord, and hover borders on drill and preset cards.
- **Blue Tone** (`marker-tone`): any other chord or scale tone.
- **Amber Reference** (`marker-reference`): the drill's reference note and prompted string. Also the focus ring (`focus`) and the notice stripe.
- **Bluish-Green Correct** (`marker-correct`): correct answers, progress bars and the active instrument.
- **Reddish-Purple Incorrect** (`marker-incorrect`): wrong answers and error notices. It is paired with a dashed stroke, and `danger-ink` is used where it appears as text.
- **Sky Revealed** (`marker-revealed`): an answer shown after a miss, and the practice-window outline.

### Named Rules
**The Instrument-Only Rule.** Wood, nickel, bone and pearl belong to the drawn neck and the brand mark. They never become UI chrome, backgrounds or buttons.

**The Never-Colour-Alone Rule.** Every marker or state colour is paired with a second cue: a dashed or dotted stroke, an icon, a text label, or border width. Removing the colour must leave the state readable.

**The Marker Meaning Rule.** An Okabe-Ito hue carries one meaning across the whole app. Vermilion is "root/selected", bluish-green is "correct/done". Don't reuse them for decoration.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (with system-ui, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (with system-ui, sans-serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** Georgia (with Times New Roman, serif), only for Roman numerals in the key helper.

**Character:** One typeface designed for low-vision readability carries everything: plain, sturdy and slightly warm. The only exception is a serif for Roman numerals, which echoes how numerals look in theory books.

### Hierarchy
All sizes multiply `--step` (0.9rem, 1rem or 1.2rem, set by the text-size setting).
- **Hero** (700, 2.8×, 1.05, -0.025em, max 18ch): the home headline only. 2.1× on phones.
- **Display** (700, 2.6×, -0.02em): screen titles on top-level tools. 2× on phones.
- **Headline / h1** (700, 2×, 1.15): page titles.
- **Title / h2** (700, 1.3×): section heads. h3 is 1.05×.
- **Prompt** (700, 1.9×): the drill question, the biggest text in the drill flow.
- **Body** (400, 1×, 1.5): running text. Lede and prose are 1.05×, capped at 62–68ch.
- **Label** (700, 0.9rem): form legends and field labels. Tags and rail labels are 0.8rem, 0.7rem on the phone tab bar.

### Named Rules
**The One-Step Rule.** Type sizes are multiples of `--step`, never fixed px or rem, so the user's text-size setting scales everything. (The current exceptions are fretboard SVG labels at 12–14px and the 0.7–0.9rem labels.)

**The Tabular Rule.** Counts, tunings, fret ranges and stats use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.

## Layout

The app shell is a CSS grid: a 56px top bar across the top, then a 96px icon rail beside the main column. Main content has `clamp(16px, 4vw, 48px)` side padding and 96px of bottom padding. Pages cap at 1100px, forms at 760px, the instrument picker at 900px, and prose at 68ch. Collections use auto-fill grids with 200–280px minimum columns and 10px gaps. Controls go in wrapping flex rows with 12px × 20px gaps.

At 720px and below, the rail becomes a fixed bottom tab bar (FR-047), the shell collapses to one column, and main padding becomes 16px. The fretboard scrolls sideways inside its own container with a sticky nut column, edge cues and a "Jump to frets" row. The page itself never scrolls sideways.

Spacing is an informal 4px-based set (4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24, 28). The `spacing` tokens record the values used most.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Depth comes from tone instead of shadow: a slate bar sits above a paper canvas, and white or night-surface cards sit on that, each with a 1px hairline. The one place `box-shadow` appears is the active rail item, as an inset 3px vermilion bar (an indicator, not a raised surface). On the fretboard, the SVG wood, frets and strings give the only sense of material depth.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Bench Rule.** Nothing floats. Use a border, a tonal surface step or a marker colour instead of a drop shadow.

## Shapes

There are two radii. Small (6px) is for controls: buttons, inputs, segmented controls and tone chips. Large (14px) is for containers: cards, preset tiles, lesson and drill tiles, and empty states. Tags, chips, step pills and progress bars are full pills. Borders are always 1px hairlines, rising to 2px only to signal state (active gear, selected chord, root tone). Dashed borders mean "custom" or "missed". Dotted means "pending".

## Components

### Buttons
Sturdy and plain.
- **Shape:** small radius (6px), at least 44 × 44px.
- **Default:** surface fill, hairline border, ink text. Hover darkens the border to soft ink.
- **Primary:** Okabe Blue fill and border, accent-ink text, bold.
- **Big:** 12px × 28px padding at 1.1× step, for the main call to action on a screen.
- **Link / Danger:** link buttons are borderless underlined accent text. Danger buttons use `danger-ink` text with a pink hairline.
- **Disabled:** 50% opacity with a not-allowed cursor.
- **Focus:** a 3px amber outline with 2px offset, everywhere.

### Segmented controls and key ring
- A hairline-framed strip with internal hairline dividers. The selected segment inverts to an ink fill with paper text, in bold.
- The root picker uses slate buttons for black keys. The selected root fills vermilion with a matching 3px outline.

### Chips and tags
- **Tag:** pill, paper fill, hairline, soft ink, 0.8rem, for metadata.
- **Step pill:** pill with a hairline. The current step gets an amber border and bold ink. Correct gets green, and missed gets a dashed reddish-purple border.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** large radius (14px).
- **Background:** surface on paper.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** 1px hairline. Hover on a navigational card turns the border accent or vermilion.
- **Internal Padding:** 14–20px.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** surface fill, hairline, small radius, 44px minimum height. Checkboxes and radios are 20px with an accent tint.
- **Focus:** the global amber ring.
- **Error:** `danger-ink` bold text. The notice gets a reddish-purple side stripe.

### Navigation
- **Top bar:** slate, brand mark plus wordmark on the left, text links (My Gear, Settings) on the right. Active and hover links get a 12% white wash.
- **Rail:** icon above a 0.8rem label. The active item gets the surface fill, ink text and an inset vermilion bar on the left. On phones it becomes a bottom tab bar, and the bar moves to the top edge.

### Fretboard (signature component)
An SVG neck drawn to the active `InstrumentProfile` (ADR-0003): rosewood board, bone nut, nickel frets, string lines, pearl inlays and fret numbers. Markers are circles: filled for tone, root, reference, correct and incorrect; outlined and dashed for revealed. Each carries a white or ink note label, plus a stroke pattern so the state reads without colour. Hit areas are transparent, with a white wash on hover and an amber ring on focus. A practice window is a dashed sky rectangle. On narrow screens the neck scrolls inside its frame, with a sticky nut column, chevron edge cues and a range readout.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every new colour from the tokens above. Add a token to `:root` and both dark blocks before using a new value.
- **Do** pair every state colour with a stroke style, icon or text label (Never-Colour-Alone).
- **Do** keep every interactive target at least 44 × 44px.
- **Do** size type as a multiple of `--step` so the text-size setting reaches it.
- **Do** draw instrument geometry from the `InstrumentProfile`, never a fixed 6 strings or fret count.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add drop shadows, glows or gradients. Depth is tonal (Flat Bench).
- **Don't** use rosewood, nickel or pearl outside the drawn instrument and the brand mark.
- **Don't** reuse a marker hue for something unrelated to its meaning.
- **Don't** add a second accent colour or a second sans typeface.
- **Don't** use a one-sided coloured stripe on a rounded box (the current `.notice` does this; it's flagged for replacement with a full border or tinted background).
